录音、转写和播放

保存录音后打开记录播放原音。文字缺失时先确认原音可播放，再检查权限、系统语言和处理状态；保留原音后尝试重新处理。

总结与说话人

总结与说话人标签可能出错，请结合原文核对。苹果端侧模型受设备支持限制；可选外部 AI 需要自行配置提供商和 API Key，服务费用另计。

Apple Watch 同步

保存后从手机时间线的手表录音筛选和同步管理查看状态。传输受系统、电量和连接状态影响；确认手机收到且能播放之前，请保留手表上的原音。

待办与日历

确认待办时间和地点后联动系统日历。从 Echo 隐藏日历展示不会删除系统日程。可在系统设置调整麦克风、语音识别、日历、健康和定位权限。

备份与恢复

从设置的数据管理导出完整备份，恢复前确认文件。录音、导出文件和已分享的副本需要分别管理。

联系支持

邮件提供应用版本与构建号、设备与系统版本、复现步骤及发生时间。不要发送密码或 API Key；录音和健康信息仅在你明确愿意分享时提供。

Support

Recording and transcription

Save the recording, then open it to play the original audio. If text is missing, check audio playback, permissions, system language and processing status. Keep the original before retrying.

Summaries and speakers

Verify summaries and speaker labels against the transcript. Apple on-device models depend on device eligibility. Optional external AI requires your own provider and API key; provider charges are separate.

Watch, calendar and backups

Check the Watch recording filter in the phone timeline and sync management after saving. Keep the original on your Watch until the phone copy plays. Hiding a calendar event in Echo does not delete the system event. Export backups from data management in Settings and verify files before restoring.

Contact support

Email tendazlkj@gmail.com with app/build version, device, OS version and reproduction steps. Do not send passwords or API keys. Share private recordings or health information only if you choose to do so.
