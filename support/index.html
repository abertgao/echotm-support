Missing or incomplete text

First check that the original audio plays. Check microphone and speech permissions, the selected language and the processing status. Keep the original audio before retrying.

AI setup and costs

Apple on-device capabilities depend on your device and language. External text AI is optional and requires your own provider configuration and API key. Its costs are separate from any app purchase.

Watch synchronization

Keep the recording on your Watch until you confirm the iPhone copy is received and playable. Check the Watch connection and sync status in the app.

Tasks and Calendar

Verify the task, time, owner and source before acting. Explicit Calendar actions can create or update system events. Hiding an event from Echo does not delete the system event.

Backups and deletion

Export a complete backup from Settings before restoring or removing important data. Manage exported files and shared copies separately.

Contact us

Email the version/build, device, OS, steps and time of the problem. Never send passwords or API keys. Only attach a recording or health information if you explicitly choose to share it.
