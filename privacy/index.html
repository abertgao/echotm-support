Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Echo / Echotm 0.3.5

Local recordings

Recordings, transcripts, summaries, tasks and settings are stored on your device. Paired Watch and iPhone exchange records through Apple device connectivity. Echo does not require an Echo account or operate an Echo business cloud server.

Permissions and context

Microphone access supports recordings you start. Speech recognition requests require on-device recognition; availability depends on your device and language. Optional calendar access supports event display and explicit calendar actions. Hiding an event in Echo does not delete the system event. Optional Health access reads heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and workouts for personal context; the app does not write Health data. Mood labels are not medical diagnoses. Optional location features may send coordinates to Apple geocoding services to obtain place names.

Optional models and network services

Compatible devices can use Apple on-device models. Downloading local speech or speaker models requires network access; download hosts may receive IP addresses and other network information, but downloading models does not upload recordings. If you configure and enable an external text AI provider, relevant record text, conversations and context, potentially including body-state or place labels, may be sent to that provider. Original audio is not uploaded as part of text model requests. API keys are stored in the device Keychain. Provider terms govern retention, processing locations and use; we cannot promise that all providers have identical privacy practices.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Exports, backups, sharing and workflow connections send content to destinations you choose. Cloud storage is governed by the selected service. Delete records and audio in the app and manage exported backups separately. Local deletion does not delete copies already exported, shared or sent to providers. Uninstalling may remove container data; Keychain items and exported files have separate lifecycles.

Advertising and tracking

The current app does not integrate advertising or advertising-tracking SDKs. Optional external services still involve the processing described above.
