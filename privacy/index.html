Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Saynary 0.3.5

Local recordings

Recordings, transcripts, summaries, tasks and settings are stored on your device. Paired Watch and iPhone exchange records through Apple device connectivity. Saynary does not require an account or store recordings on its own servers.

Permissions and context

Microphone access supports recordings you start. Built-in speech recognition requests on-device processing. Availability depends on your device and language. Optional calendar access supports event display and explicit calendar actions. Hiding an event in Echo does not delete the system event. Optional Health access reads heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and workouts for personal context; the app does not write Health data. Mood labels are not medical diagnoses. Optional location features may send coordinates to Apple geocoding services to obtain place names.

Optional models and network services

Compatible devices can use Apple models on the device. Hosts that deliver local speech or speaker models may receive your IP address and related network information. Model downloads do not upload your recordings. If you enable an external text AI provider, relevant transcripts, record text, questions and conversation history can be sent to the provider you chose. Personal vocabulary may be included when the provider corrects a transcript. Original audio, measurements from Apple Health, labels derived from those measurements and automatic place labels are excluded from text model requests. Voiceprints and speaker profiles are stored on the device. Names already present in record text may be sent along with that text. API keys are stored in the device Keychain and sent to the configured provider to authenticate its requests. Each provider sets its own processing locations and retention rules. You can turn off external text AI or remove its key in Settings.

Optional Qwen transcription

If you set up Qwen, the connection test sends the URL of a public sample, along with your Alibaba Cloud API key for authentication. When you approve Qwen for a recording, or separately approve Qwen as the default for eligible future recordings, Saynary sends a converted copy of the recording directly to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio in the region you select (Beijing, China, or Singapore). It returns text, timestamps and speaker labels such as “Speaker 1”. You can choose an on-device default or remove the Qwen key in Settings to stop future automatic uploads. Alibaba Cloud describes default retention of model inputs and outputs for up to 30 days, subject to exceptions and eligible workspace settings, in its data retention policy. Deleting a local recording does not erase any copy already sent to a provider.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Exports, backups, sharing and workflow connections send content to destinations you choose. Cloud storage is governed by the selected service. Delete records and audio in the app and manage exported backups separately. Local deletion does not delete copies already exported, shared or sent to providers. Uninstalling may remove container data; Keychain items and exported files have separate lifecycles.

Advertising and tracking

The current app does not integrate advertising or advertising-tracking SDKs. Optional external services still involve the processing described above.
